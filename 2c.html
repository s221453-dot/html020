Why Two's Complement Won Computing

The Dual Zero Dilemma

Sign-Magnitude wastes a bit pattern on negative zero (1000), meaning +0 and -0 both exist. This causes complex conditional checks in hardware ALUs.

Two's Complement: Has only one zero (0000), reclaiming that extra bit pattern to store one more negative number!
⚖️

Asymmetric Ranges (N-bits)

For n bits:

  • Sign-Magnitude: -(2^(n-1)-1) to +(2^(n-1)-1)
  • Two's Complement: -2^(n-1) to +(2^(n-1)-1)
4-Bit Range: SM: -7 to +7 | 2's Comp: -8 to +7
⚡

Hardware Arithmetic Simplicity

In Two's Complement, subtraction is performed simply by negating the subtrahend and adding! Standard adder circuits handle both positive and negative numbers without extra sign logic.

Result: Faster processors, fewer logic gates, and streamlined CPU architecture.
